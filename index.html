About the Fest

What is Sthapatya?

STHAPATYA is the flagship annual technical symposium organized by the Civil Engineering Students Association (CiESA) at PCCOE, Pune.

It brings together engineering students from across colleges to showcase technical knowledge, creativity, problem-solving, and practical engineering abilities through hands-on competitions — from campus-wide treasure hunts to professional AutoCAD drafting battles and 3D printing showdowns.

Whether you're mapping survey grids, decoding investigation clues, or designing NBC-compliant floor plans — Sthapatya is where blueprints come alive and concrete meets code.
