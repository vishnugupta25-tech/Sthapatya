About the Fest

What is STHAPATYA?

STHAPATYA is the flagship annual technical symposium organized by the Civil Engineering Students Association (CiESA) at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune.

It brings together engineering students, innovators, and builders from across colleges to showcase their technical knowledge, creativity, problem-solving skills, and practical engineering abilities through hands-on competitions, treasure hunts, 3D printing challenges, and professional drafting battles.

Whether you're decoding clues across campus or drafting NBC-compliant floor plans — STHAPATYA is where blueprints come alive.
