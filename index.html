What is Sthapatya?

STHAPATYA is the flagship annual technical symposium organized by the Civil Engineering Students Association (CiESA) at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune.

It brings together future civil engineers, innovators, and structural designers from across colleges to showcase technical knowledge, surveying accuracy, architectural drafting precision, and structural engineering capabilities through hands-on competitions.

Whether you're calculating theodolite traverses, decoding forensic site clues, or drafting NBC-compliant building elevations — Sthapatya is where blueprints come alive and concrete meets code.

🧭 Surveying & Geodesy
Benchmarking, fly levelling, theodolite traverse computations, total station precision sighting, and ground contour mapping.

🖨️ Additive Civil Tech
Computational 3D modeling, slicing optimization, and physical prototype fabrication bridging digital design to tangible civil scale models.
