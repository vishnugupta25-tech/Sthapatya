## 1. Challenge Overview & Problem Statement

### 1.1 Objective
> **"Develop a complete building design from the given problem statement, ensuring functional planning and standard compliance."**

Participants are tasked with synthesizing an end-to-end architectural and engineering drawing set on AutoCAD. The design must resolve space planning constraints, structural rationality, comprehensive MEP service integration, statutory bye-law compliance (FSI, setbacks, light & ventilation), and technical drawing conventions.

### 1.2 Given Problem Statement & Client Brief
- **Plot Dimensions:** $12.00\text{ m}$ (Frontage) $\times 18.00\text{ m}$ (Depth) = **$216.00\text{ m}^2$ (2,325 sq.ft)**
- **Orientation:** North-facing plot abutting a $9.00\text{ m}$ wide municipal access road.
- **Typology:** G+1 Contemporary Residential Villa / Duplex for a nuclear family of 5 members.
- **Required Accommodation:**
  - **Ground Floor:** Entrance Foyer/Verandah, Living Hall, Dining Area, Modular Kitchen with Utility/Store, Guest Bedroom with En-suite, Common Powder Room/Toilet, Staircase, Covered Car Porch.
  - **First Floor:** Master Bedroom with Walk-in Wardrobe & En-suite Toilet, Children's Bedroom with En-suite Toilet, Family Lounge / Study, Open Terrace / Balconies.

## 3. Detailed Technical Modules

### Module 1: Problem Statement Analysis & Space Planning
- **Zoning Hierarchy:**
  - *Public Zone:* Entrance Porch, Foyer, Living Hall (located North/North-East for diffused daylight).
  - *Semi-Private Zone:* Dining Room, Kitchen, Utility (South-East for Vastu & early morning sun).
  - *Private Zone:* Bedrooms and Baths (South & South-West for privacy and prevailing wind capture).
- **Circulation Efficiency:** Total circulation area kept $\le 15\%$ of gross carpet area.
- **Aspect & Prospect:**
  - Living & Master Bedroom oriented toward front garden and open North-East sky.
  - Kitchen located on South-East to avoid evening heat buildup and optimize chimney exhaust.

### Module 4: Plumbing Layout & Service Connections
- **Drainage Scheme (Two-Pipe System as per IS 1742 / NBC Part 9):**
  - **Soil Pipe (Black Water):** $100\text{ mm} \;\phi$ cast iron / UPVC pipe discharging water closet wastes directly to municipal inspection chamber through gully-bypassed connection.
  - **Waste Pipe (Grey Water):** $75\text{ mm} \;\phi$ UPVC pipe carrying bath, washbasin, and kitchen sink effluent through deep-seal floor traps ($P$-trap / $S$-trap with $50\text{ mm}$ water seal) into external gully traps.
  - **Vent Pipe:** $50\text{ mm} \;\phi$ anti-siphonage pipe terminating $1.00\text{ m}$ above terrace parapet with cowl cap.
- **Slope & Inspection Chambers:**
  - Underground sewer lines laid to gradient: $1:50$ (minimum self-cleansing velocity $0.75\text{ m/s}$).
  - $450\text{ mm} \times 450\text{ mm}$ brick masonry Inspection Chambers (IC-1 to IC-4) provided at every change of direction and connection junction.
- **Water Supply Network:**
  - Underground Sump Tank: $4,500\text{ Litres}$ (RCC water-retaining structure with waterproofing).
  - Overhead Tank (OHT): $2,500\text{ Litres}$ dual-compartment HDPE / RCC tank on terrace tower.
  - Consumption Standard: $135\text{ LPCD}$ (Litres per capita per day as per IS 1172 for residential dwelling).
  - Ring-main distribution with gate valves, ball valves, and non-return valves (NRV).

### Module 7: Construction Notes & Material Specifications

1. **Substructure & Foundation:**
   - Isolated stepped RCC column footings resting on hard strata (safe bearing capacity $\ge 200\text{ kN/m}^2$ at depth $-1.80\text{ m}$).
   - Concrete grade: **M25** (characteristic compressive strength $f_{ck} = 25\text{ N/mm}^2$).
   - Reinforcement: High-yield strength deformed TMT bars of grade **Fe500** conforming to IS 1786.
2. **Plinth & Damp Proof Course (DPC):**
   - RCC plinth beam ($230\text{ mm} \times 300\text{ mm}$) tie along all column axes.
   - Continuous $50\text{ mm}$ thick DPC of M20 concrete with approved water-repellent admixture (CICO / Dr. Fixit) coated with 2 applications of hot blown bitumen (grade 85/25) at $+0.60\text{ m}$ plinth level.
3. **Superstructure Masonry:**
   - Exterior walls: First-class burnt clay bricks conforming to IS 1077 (crushing strength $\ge 7.5\text{ N/mm}^2$) laid in cement-sand mortar $1:6$.
   - Internal partition walls: $115\text{ mm}$ brickwork laid in cement mortar $1:4$ with $6\text{ mm}$ MS wire ties embedded every 4th course.
4. **Plastering & Finishes:**
   - External: $20\text{ mm}$ thick two-coat sand-faced cement plaster (base coat $12\text{ mm}$ in $1:4$, finish coat $8\text{ mm}$ in $1:3$) with silicone exterior emulsion paint.
   - Internal: $12\text{ mm}$ thick cement plaster in $1:6$ with smooth lime/neeru finish and premium acrylic emulsion.
5. **Terrace Waterproofing:**
   - Brick bat coba waterproofing ($100\text{ mm}$ to $120\text{ mm}$ average thickness) laid to slope of $1:80$ toward rainwater outlets with china-mosaic upper finish for solar reflectance.
